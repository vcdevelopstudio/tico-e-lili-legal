Privacy Policy

Updated October 2, 2026

Developer/publisher: VC Develop Studio

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains the practices of the current version of Tico e Lili — Descobrindo a Fauna e Flora do Brasil, intended mainly for children aged 3–5, with content in Portuguese, English and Spanish. Parents and guardians can learn how the game stores progress, how to erase local data and how to contact us. Visits to this document website are covered separately in section 8.

2. Personal information and children

The game does not require its own account, show ads or use usage analytics or behavioral tracking. It does not request a child's name, email address, age, photo, location or identifiers, or use the camera or microphone. The publisher does not receive game progress or preferences and does not sell or share personal information obtained during play.

3. What stays on the device

The game locally saves the selected language, music, sound-effect and narration volumes, completed stages, revealed rewards and book-page status. These records let you continue the journey, keep settings and identify content already viewed. The device language is checked to initially select Portuguese, English or Spanish; if it does not match one of those languages, the game uses English. A saved manual selection takes priority afterward.

Records stay in the app's data area. The publisher does not keep a server copy or provide synchronization between devices. When an update changes how progress is organized, a local copy of the previous save file may be created for migration.

4. Narration and services used by the game

Music, sound effects and narration play from files included in the app. Book narration does not send text to an online voice service or use device text-to-speech during play. The current version does not integrate advertising, usage analytics, online-account services or Google Play Billing, and does not send local records to external services.

The operating system and store used to install or update the app may process data under their own settings and policies. This does not constitute the game sending progress. Any backups or restores performed by the system depend on the device and the account configured by the family.

5. Planned purchase

A one-time Google Play purchase is planned to grant access to stages 4–10. This integration has not been implemented. The current version does not process purchases or receive purchase data through Google Play Billing. Before this feature becomes available, this policy will be updated to describe the data and services actually used. The plan does not mean that purchasing is already available.

6. Retention, resetting and local deletion

Progress and preferences remain on the device while the app's files are kept. In Settings, “Reset progress” requires confirmation through an arithmetic question intended for an adult. A correct answer followed by confirmation clears stage progress, rewards and book-page status; only stage 1 becomes available again. Language and all three volume settings remain saved.

Resetting progress does not delete any local copy created for migration. To also remove preferences and the app's other local files, use the clear storage/data option in Android settings. Uninstalling and restoring system backups follow the device settings. The publisher cannot remotely erase device files or recover progress that has been removed.

7. Support and privacy contact

Parents or guardians should contact vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com. If you send a message, we receive your email address and whatever content you choose to send so we can respond to your request. This is separate from the game's local data. Avoid including a child's name, photo or other personal information unless needed to explain the issue.

Messages are handled by the email service used for support and your email provider under their policies. We use this information to handle the request and retain it for as long as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. You can use the same contact to request information, correction or deletion of submitted data, subject to any retention obligations. There is no game account to delete.

8. Visits to the document website

This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages do not require login, and the site's code does not add ads, usage analytics, cookies or browser storage for preferences. Your language choice may appear in the page address.

When visiting the site, your browser communicates with GitHub to receive its pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, even without login. This hosting activity is separate from the game's operation and follows GitHub's policies and retention periods. See the GitHub Pages data collection documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Security and changes

Journey data stays in the app's local data area; its protection also depends on the device's security and access controls. The document website uses HTTPS. Support messages are transmitted and stored by the email providers involved, under those services' protections.

When the practices of the game or these contact channels change, this policy and its update date will be revised. Introducing the planned purchase requires another review of actual practices before making it available. For questions about this policy, contact vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com.
