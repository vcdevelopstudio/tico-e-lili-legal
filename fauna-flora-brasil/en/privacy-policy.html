Privacy Policy

Updated October 8, 2026

Developer/publisher: VC Develop Studio

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains the practices of the current version of Tico e Lili — Descobrindo a Fauna e Flora do Brasil, intended mainly for children aged 3–5, with content in Portuguese, English and Spanish. Parents and guardians can learn how the game stores progress, how to erase local data and how to contact us. Visits to this document website are covered separately in section 8.

2. Personal information and children

The game does not require its own account, show ads, have a publisher backend or include its own gameplay analytics or behavioral tracking SDK. It does not request a child’s name, email address, age, photo or location, or use the camera or microphone. Progress and preferences are not sent to the publisher. This does not mean that third-party services collect no data: Google Play Billing processes purchase data and automatically sends technical diagnostics, even without a purchase, as explained in sections 4 and 5.

3. What stays on the device

The game locally saves the selected language, music, sound-effect and narration volumes, completed stages, revealed rewards and book-page status. These records let you continue the journey, keep settings and identify content already viewed. The device language is checked to initially select Portuguese, English or Spanish; if it does not match one of those languages, the game uses English. A saved manual selection takes priority afterward.

Records stay in the app's data area. The publisher does not keep a server copy or provide synchronization between devices. When an update changes how progress is organized, a local copy of the previous save file may be created for migration.

Separately from progress, the game’s own purchase-entitlement cache stores the product, app package and access state, without tokens or banking data. The diagnostic library maintains its own persistent local queue for the technical transmission described in section 4.

4. Narration, third-party services and technical diagnostics

Music, sound effects and narration play from files included in the app. Book narration does not send text to an online voice service or use device text-to-speech during play.

The game integrates Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0. It starts automatically and collects and shares technical diagnostics with Google through DataTransport/CCT, even when no one makes a purchase. Records include technical app and device metadata and a temporary library session identifier. The documented purposes are diagnosing issues, improving performance and supporting error resolution; this is separate from local progress and preferences and is not the game’s own gameplay analytics SDK.

Diagnostic transport uses HTTPS and a persistent local queue. Processing therefore does not take place only temporarily in memory (it is non-ephemeral). Collection and sharing of diagnostics and the temporary identifier are mandatory in the implemented behavior: the game has no option to disable this telemetry, and not purchasing does not turn it off. Classifying these data as Diagnostics and Device or other IDs, for Analytics, is a reasoned interpretation of the general Google Play Data safety rules, not a specific confirmation by the Google Billing team.

Complete anonymization of these records has not been established. The temporary session identifier should not be understood as a guarantee of anonymity. The practices described do not demonstrate use of an advertising ID, Android ID, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

Google processes data from its services under the Google Privacy Policy. The operating system and store may also process installation, update, backup and restore data under their own settings and policies. This does not constitute the game sending progress to the publisher.

5. Purchases, purchase queries and restoration through Google Play

Stages 1–3 are free. A one-time, non-consumable purchase grants the right to access stages 4–10. There is no subscription or recurring charge. Purchasing does not skip progression: stages continue to unlock as the player advances. Google Play processes payment. The purchase area has a numeric challenge intended for a parent or guardian; it is not legal age or identity verification.

The integration implements purchasing, querying and restoring existing purchases, and acknowledging transactions. For these functions, the game receives purchase information, including a reference/token, state and product, and sends the token to Google Play to acknowledge the transaction. Its own cache stores the product, app package and access-entitlement state; it does not store tokens or banking data. The game’s code does not access card or banking details and does not send purchase data to a publisher backend.

Purchase history is collected for app functionality in connection with an optional purchase or querying/restoring an existing entitlement, and is not processed only ephemerally. In the Data safety classification, purchase-history collection is optional and its purpose is App functionality. Sharing is not declared under the exception for expected processing of a user-initiated purchase/restoration. This interpretation of the general rules is not a specific confirmation by the Google Billing team and does not mean that no data is sent to Google to process these actions. It does not apply to the automatic diagnostics in section 4.

Implementing these functions does not establish that purchasing or restoration has been validated on a device or specifically approved by Google. This document does not claim that such tests or approvals have occurred.

6. Retention, resetting and local deletion

Progress and preferences remain on the device while the app's files are kept. In Settings, “Reset progress” requires confirmation through an arithmetic question intended for an adult. A correct answer followed by confirmation clears stage progress, rewards and book-page status; only stage 1 becomes available again. Language and all three volume settings remain saved.

Resetting progress does not delete any local copy created for migration. To also remove preferences and the app's other local files, use the clear storage/data option in Android settings. Uninstalling and restoring system backups follow the device settings. The publisher cannot remotely erase device files or recover progress that has been removed.

The numeric challenge is a barrier intended for a parent or guardian, not legal age or identity verification. Resetting progress does not cancel the purchase, remove the purchase entitlement linked to Google Play or delete purchase records or diagnostics processed by Google. The access entitlement is separate from stage progress.

Clearing storage/data also removes the game’s own access-entitlement cache and the local diagnostic queue; it does not cancel the purchase or erase data already sent to Google. Querying/restoring the entitlement depends on Google Play records and may require a connection and the account used for purchase. The publisher does not control retention or deletion on Google’s servers and has no confirmed retention period for these diagnostics or guarantee of automatic deletion. Requests about data processed by Google should follow that service’s privacy channels and controls.

7. Support and privacy contact

Parents or guardians should contact vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com. If you send a message, we receive your email address and whatever content you choose to send so we can respond to your request. This is separate from the game's local data. Avoid including a child's name, photo or other personal information unless needed to explain the issue.

Messages are handled by the email service used for support and your email provider under their policies. We use this information to handle the request and retain it for as long as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. You can use the same contact to request information, correction or deletion of submitted data, subject to any retention obligations. There is no game account to delete.

8. Visits to the document website

This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages do not require login, and the site's code does not add ads, usage analytics, cookies or browser storage for preferences. Your language choice may appear in the page address.

When visiting the site, your browser communicates with GitHub to receive its pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, even without login. This hosting activity is separate from the game's operation and follows GitHub's policies and retention periods. See the GitHub Pages data collection documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Security and changes

Journey data and the access-entitlement cache stay in the app’s local data area; their protection also depends on the device’s security and access controls. Technical diagnostic transport uses HTTPS and a persistent local queue. The document website uses HTTPS. Support messages are transmitted and stored by the email providers involved, under those services’ protections.

When the practices of the game or these contact channels change, this policy and its update date will be revised. For questions about this policy, contact vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com.
