Privacy Policy

Updated October 13, 2026

Publisher: VCDevelopStudio

1. About this policy

This policy addresses parents and guardians of children using Tico e Lili : Uma Viagem Pelo Espaço, an Android space exploration game designed for ages 3–5 and also enjoyable at age 6. It describes the approved final product model. Purchase integration is still in development; publishing these documents does not announce app availability, Google approval or implemented purchases. Before commercial availability, this policy will be reviewed against the services and package actually integrated.

2. Children and personal data

The approved model does not include child registration, a studio account, advertising, chat or sending progress to the studio. The planned activities do not require names, email addresses, dates of birth, identity documents, photos or location. The adult area uses a mathematical challenge as a practical navigation barrier; it does not legally verify age, identity or consent. Guardians should supervise device use and store account controls.

3. Local progress and preferences

The model provides for on-device storage of completed stages, unlocked or revealed keepsakes, unlocked or viewed pages, language, and music, effects and narration volumes. These records support continuation of the journey and the family's choices; there is no checkpoint for an unfinished attempt. Progress synchronization with a studio server is not planned. The initial language follows the device's Portuguese, English or Spanish, with English as fallback; manual choices are retained. Audio files are to be included in the installation without relying on online narration.

4. Purchases and store services

The final model provides free stages 1–3 and a permanent one-time purchase for access to stages 4–10, without subscriptions or advertising. Buying, restoring and checking the current price require internet and store services; purchase and restoration go through the adult area. The store will process payment under its terms and privacy policy. Recognizing and restoring access rights will require the commercial integration to handle transaction information and its status. The Space game's SDK, version, received fields, storage and diagnostic settings are not yet confirmed. We do not claim that the app already receives tokens, maintains a particular cache or starts queries at launch. Nor do we claim that the store or its SDK collects no data simply because play is local. This policy will be updated with these details before commercial delivery. Google services may process account, payment and technical information under their policies.

5. Offline use and limitations

The planned content is included in the installation and allows play without additional downloads. After a purchase is recognized, keeping access to paid stages offline depends on a persistent local record and validation in the app; this behavior has not yet been confirmed. Offline purchasing, restoration or price queries are not promised. Usage analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs have not been confirmed for the Space game; we do not attribute other games' services to it. An unconfirmed integration is not a traffic audit or a guarantee that no data is processed anywhere.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records are retained for game continuity. In the approved model, resetting progress in the protected adult area clears achievements, completions, keepsakes and pages while preserving language, volumes and purchase rights. Restoring a purchase recovers access, not deleted progress. Clearing app storage in Android settings removes local app data; uninstalling or changing devices may cause progress loss. This does not cancel the purchase in the store account. System backup, recovery copies and commercial cache behavior have not yet been confirmed; file recovery is not promised. Local deletion does not erase store records, which must be handled through its channels.

7. Support and privacy rights

VCDevelopStudio is responsible for the product. When you email vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com, you provide your email address and chosen content. We use this data to handle requests, including access, correction or deletion of information received by the studio, under applicable law. Messages are kept for the time needed for support and relevant legal obligations, without an unconfirmed fixed deadline. Gmail and the sender's email provider process messages under their policies. Avoid sending children's names, photos or other data unnecessary for support. The studio does not offer remote recovery of local progress and cannot directly delete store-server records.

8. Visits to this website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and are accessible without login. The Space pages load no advertising or usage analytics scripts and store no cookies or preferences in the browser. Connecting to the hosting provider is separate from game operation. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, even without login. Opening browser links may require internet.

We do not sell data sent to support. On-device protection also depends on the system, updates and family controls; absolute security is not promised. Store, email and hosting services process data under their policies. We will review this document when app practices are confirmed or change. The date above identifies this edition. Portuguese, English and Spanish versions should be read with the Terms of Use; English is the default for public references without a specified language.
