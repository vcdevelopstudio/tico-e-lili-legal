Privacy Policy

Updated on November 14, 2026

Publisher: VCDevelopStudio

1. About this policy

This policy is for the parents and guardians of children who use Tico e Lili : Uma Viagem Pelo Espaço, a space exploration game for Android, package com.vcdevelopstudio.ticoeliliespaco, designed for children aged 3–5 and also fun at 6. It describes commercial version 0.9.0, which integrates purchasing through Google Play. Publishing this policy does not announce the app’s availability or Google’s approval, and does not change versions already installed.

2. Children and personal data

The game has no child registration, studio account, ads, chat, camera, microphone, location or sending of progress to the studio. No name, email, date of birth, documents or photos are needed. Purchasing and opening other apps sit behind a math challenge for adults, with a voice that calls an adult; it is a practical navigation barrier and does not legally verify age, identity or consent. We do not integrate any service that analyzes play behavior. Google’s purchasing services have their own data practices, described below.

3. Local progress and preferences

Completed phases, unlocked or revealed memories, unlocked or viewed pages, language and music, effects and narration volumes stay on the device. These records let the family continue the journey and keep its choices; there is no checkpoint for an unfinished attempt and no sync with a studio server. The initial language follows the device’s Portuguese, English or Spanish, with English as the fallback; a manual choice is kept. Music, effects and narration, including the voice that calls an adult, are files included in the app.

4. One-time purchase and restore

Phases 1–3 are free. A single, permanent, non-consumable purchase gives access to phases 4–10, with no subscription and no ads. When a paid phase is chosen, the adult answers the challenge and sees the offer, with pictures of the phases, the localized price retrieved from Google Play, Buy and Restore. Google Play processes the payment; the app has no access to card data. The integration receives the product, purchase state and token and the acknowledgement status to grant access and acknowledge the transaction (ACK) with the store; these data are handled in memory. A pending purchase only grants access after the store confirms it. The local purchase record keeps only the product code, the package and the access flag, with no token, account, email, card data or order ID, and is separate from progress. There is no studio validation server. The commercial version starts Billing and checks price and purchases when the app opens, when it returns to the app and when the map opens, and checks again when the guardian taps Restore. Buying, checking the price and restoring require internet and the Google account used for the purchase.

5. Google services diagnostics

The commercial version integrates GodotGooglePlayBilling 3.3.0 and Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0. The library includes operational diagnostics sent to Google, with a persistent local DataTransport/CCT queue. This technical information may include connection, success and failure events, a random library session identifier, the package and app, library and Android versions, brand, model, manufacturer and system build identifier. This technical identifier is not the advertising ID, IMEI or serial number, and does not mean anonymity. Billing starts when the commercial app opens, before the adult area and regardless of a purchase; the game offers no option to turn these diagnostics off and not buying does not turn them off. They are distinct from analysis of play. Google handles these data under its Privacy Policy; we do not set a retention period in Google’s systems.

Google Privacy Policy

6. Offline use and limits

The content ships with the installation and can be played without extra downloads. Once the purchase is recognized, the local record keeps access to phases 4–10 without internet. A connection or store failure does not remove access; a completed check in which the store no longer reports the purchase, for example after a refund, removes local access. Buying, restoring and checking the price do not work offline. We do not integrate our own advertising, usage analytics or crash reporting SDK; we do not attribute other titles’ services to this game. This description is not a traffic audit.

7. More games

“More games” is an optional area that shows other apps from the same publisher. The public list and icons are hosted on GitHub Pages, at https://vcdevelopstudio.github.io/tico-e-lili-legal/catalogo/familia/v1/jogos.json and on the same host. Opening the list or tapping Refresh fetches that list and, when needed, downloads icons over HTTPS; there are no such requests when the game opens or during phases. These downloads happen before the adult challenge, which only protects opening the store. The host receives the IP address, the requested path and technical headers; GitHub states that it logs IP addresses for security. Requests carry no cookies, personal or advertising identifiers, purchase tokens, progress or challenge results. The catalog cache keeps the public list, its revision and validity and the icons; its content is used for at most 24 hours after acceptance. Opening another app’s store page requires an explicit choice and a correct answer to the challenge; there is no installation, automatic purchase or reward.

8. Retention and deletion

Local records are kept so the game can continue. Resetting progress, in the area protected for the guardian, erases completions, achievements, memories and pages, but keeps language, volumes, the purchase record and the catalog cache. Restoring a purchase recovers access, not erased progress. The commercial version turns off Android automatic backup; clearing the app’s data, uninstalling or changing devices removes local records, and access can be restored online with the same account. These actions do not cancel the purchase or erase Google or GitHub records, which must be handled through their channels.

9. Support and privacy rights

VCDevelopStudio is responsible for the product. When you write to vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com, you provide your email address and the content you choose. We use these data to answer requests, including requests to access, correct or delete information received by the studio, under applicable law. We keep messages for as long as needed for support and relevant legal obligations. Gmail and the sender’s email provider process messages under their own policies. Avoid sending children’s names, photos or other data not needed for support. The studio offers no remote recovery of local progress and cannot directly delete records on the store’s servers.

10. Visits to this site

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and can be opened without signing in. The Space pages load no advertising or analytics scripts and store no cookies or preferences in the browser. Opening these documents in a browser is separate from the game’s catalog downloads. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, even without sign-in.
