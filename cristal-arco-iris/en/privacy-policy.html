Privacy Policy

Updated October 9, 2026

Developer/publisher: VC Develop Studio

1. Overview

Tico e Lili: Cristal Arco-Íris was made for children and families. No integrations for advertising, behavioral analytics of play, studio-managed accounts or chat were found in the app code examined. This does not mean that SDKs collect no data: Billing technical diagnostics are described in section 15. The descriptions of app behavior in this policy are based on that code; they are not a guarantee about all processing by the external services described below. The descriptions of the local purchased-access cache and Play Age Signals apply only to versions that include these features; they do not automatically describe earlier versions that remain installed. The access cache and Play Age Signals are prepared for an update after the version 1.0.2 shown in the store; they should not be attributed to version 1.0.2 merely because they exist in the current code. Availability depends on the installed version, and this revision does not announce the release of the update.

2. Local progress and preferences

Completed stages, the current stage, language, overall volume and sound-repetition settings stay in the app data area on the device. They let you continue the journey and keep family preferences. These records are not sent to a studio server, and the game does not provide progress synchronization between devices. Versions with a purchased-access cache also have a separate local file, described in section 14; it is not a record of stage progress.

3. Google Play Billing

Google Play processes payment for the one-time purchase unlocking stages 4–10. The app uses the localized price and product and transaction metadata: product identifiers, purchase state, purchase token and acknowledgment status (whether the purchase has already been acknowledged with Google Play). This information enables the app to identify the purchase, confirm or restore access and acknowledge the purchase; the token is used for that acknowledgment. No persistence of the token in files or recording in logs was identified in the app code examined. This use should not be confused with permanent storage by the studio. Google handles payment data under its own practices; no receipt of card details by the studio was identified in the flow examined. Purchasing, querying and restoring access involve Google Play online services. The code review does not cover a complete inspection of the distributed app (AAB), SDK traffic or all Google services. The token is sent to Google Play to acknowledge the purchase. Technical diagnostics independent of this flow are described in section 15.

4. Device narration

The invitation to call an adult on the purchase screen may be narrated using device text-to-speech (TTS), when a voice compatible with the selected language is available. The game provides the invitation text to the voice engine selected by the system. The provider configured by the family may process it locally or use online services, depending on its settings and policy; we therefore do not claim that all TTS works offline. Game music and sound effects play from files included in the app. The narration does not record the child’s voice. Voice is used to call parents or guardians at the purchase gate; there is no stage narration or recording of children’s voices.

5. Children's data

The app does not directly request a child’s name, email address, identity document, date of birth, photo, location or registration. Versions with Play Age Signals may receive an age range and platform statuses, as described in section 13. The arithmetic question separates the purchase decision from play; it does not verify age, identity or legal consent. A responsible adult should supervise purchases and use Google Play account controls. Resetting progress requires confirmation intended for an adult. Purchase metadata is described in section 3, the local cache in section 14 and voluntary contact by parents and guardians in section 12.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. The game does not send local progress or preferences to the studio. Google Play Billing and the device’s configured TTS provider handle information needed for the functions described in sections 3 and 4 under their own practices. Email support and visits to the document website are separate activities described in sections 11 and 12. In versions with Play Age Signals, Google Play may provide the app with the signals described in section 13; the code examined does not send them to a studio server or use them for advertising. SDKs may send the technical diagnostics described in section 15 to Google even without a purchase. The absence of ads or a studio-owned server does not mean the absence of this processing.

7. Security

Progress and preferences stay in the app’s local data area; their protection also depends on device updates, protections and access controls. The app code review did not identify transmission of these files or the purchase token to a server owned by the studio; external services are described in sections 3 and 4. The document website uses HTTPS. Support messages are transmitted and stored by the email providers involved under those services’ protections.

8. Retention

Progress and preferences remain on the device while the app’s files are kept. Resetting progress replaces stage progress with the initial state and keeps preferences. Clearing app storage/data in Android settings removes its local files. Uninstalling and any restoration of system backups depend on device settings. The studio does not keep a copy of progress. Store, voice-provider, hosting and support-message records follow the separate handling described in this policy. In versions that include these features, the access cache remains in the local file until updated through reconciliation or removed by clearing app data, as described in section 14. Age signals are held only in memory, as described in section 13. The SDK’s local diagnostic queue, described in section 15, is separate from the access cache and age signals held in memory. Local removal does not establish deletion of records already sent to Google.

9. Deletion

In Settings, “Reset progress” requires a correct answer to an arithmetic question and confirmation before returning to stage 1 and clearing completed stages. Language, volume and other settings remain saved. This action does not cancel or delete the purchase linked to the Google Play account. To also remove preferences and other local files, use the clear app storage/data option in Android settings. The studio cannot remotely erase device data. Data held by Google Play and other providers must be managed through those services’ channels. In versions with an access cache, clearing local data also removes that cache; this does not cancel the purchase in the Google Play account. Age signals do not form a local file to delete. Clearing app data removes its local files and queues, but does not automatically erase records already sent to third parties. For messages sent to the studio, use the contact in section 12; for data handled by Google and other providers, use their channels and policies, without a guarantee of immediate deletion or an unconfirmed deadline.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services, or legal requirements change. The date at the top will be revised and material changes will appear in a new app version.

11. Visits to the document website

When visiting its pages, your browser communicates with the hosting service. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, even without login. This handling is separate from the game’s operation and follows GitHub’s policies and retention periods.

13. Play Age Signals and the purchase flow

In versions that include Play Age Signals, the app requests an age range and sharing and significant-change approval statuses from Google Play, when available. The platform may display a sharing request depending on the account, supervision and settings. The purpose is to support child protection and handle new purchases according to the statuses received, without treating the age range as proof of identity or adulthood. In the code examined, these signals are held only in memory, without files, logs or transmission to a studio server. The age range is cleared when the app is paused and queried again on return; known blocking statuses may remain in memory during the process. The app does not directly request an identity document or date of birth, or read the installation identifier or approval date that the API can return. A missing signal, refusal to share or error does not prove adulthood. Without a known explicit block, the purchase flow may continue with the adult gate and Google Play controls. A Play verification requirement or pending or declined significant-change approval blocks new purchases; a query error does not clear a block already known during the process. This block on new purchases does not erase progress or itself revoke previously purchased access, which remains subject to store confirmation. Google handles account, age and supervision information under its own policies.

14. Local purchased-access cache

In versions that include this feature, the app stores the product identifier, format version and a flag indicating access previously confirmed by Google Play locally. The file is separate from progress and preferences and allows continued access to purchased stages when reopening the game offline, if the cache was saved successfully. This cache contains no card details, purchase token or order identifier. It is not a store account, does not synchronize progress between devices and is not tamper-proof. When connectivity and services are available, the app queries Google Play to reconcile access. A successful query with a valid list containing no confirmed or pending purchase of the product disables local access to paid stages; a network failure or incomplete response does not remove previously confirmed access. Google Play may take time to update its information, and revocation is not detected while the device is offline. Progress remains saved. Clearing app data also removes the cache; recovering access depends on fresh confirmation from the store under the appropriate account, without automatically requiring another purchase.

15. Billing technical diagnostics

The integration examined uses Google Play Billing 9.1.0 and its diagnostic transport. These components record API operation events, such as successes, failures and connection issues, for diagnosis and service improvement. The transport may add technical app and device information, such as versions, model, operating system, network type, language and locale. The library also uses technical client-instance and operation identifiers; this does not establish that they are advertising identifiers or anonymous data. Records are forwarded to Google services and may remain in a persistent local disk queue for later transmission. This flow is therefore not described as memory-only or ephemeral processing. In the code reviewed, the Billing connection starts when the app initializes with monetization active, before a purchase or an answer to the parental challenge. Not buying does not mean disabling these diagnostics. This processing is separate from card details collected directly by Google Play, the access cache and behavioral analytics of play. The description is based on the library and code examined, not a complete traffic capture or inspection of the currently distributed package. No retention period, anonymization or deletion of these records on Google servers has been confirmed; we do not promise these conditions.
