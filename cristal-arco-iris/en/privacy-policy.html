Privacy Policy

Updated October 3, 2026

Developer/publisher: VC Develop Studio

1. Overview

Tico e Lili: Cristal Arco-Íris was made for children and families. No integrations for advertising, analytics, studio-managed accounts or chat were found in the app code examined. The descriptions of app behavior in this policy are based on that code; they are not a guarantee about all processing by the external services described below.

2. Local progress and preferences

Completed stages, the current stage, language, overall volume and sound-repetition settings stay in the app data area on the device. They let you continue the journey and keep family preferences. These records are not sent to a studio server, and the game does not provide progress synchronization between devices.

3. Google Play Billing

Google Play processes payment for the one-time purchase unlocking stages 4–10. The app uses the localized price and product and transaction metadata: product identifiers, purchase state, purchase token and acknowledgment status (whether the purchase has already been acknowledged with Google Play). This information enables the app to identify the purchase, confirm or restore access and acknowledge the purchase; the token is used for that acknowledgment. No persistence of the token in files or recording in logs was identified in the app code examined. This use should not be confused with permanent storage by the studio. Google handles payment data under its own practices; no receipt of card details by the studio was identified in the flow examined. Purchasing, querying and restoring access involve Google Play online services. The code review does not cover a complete inspection of the distributed app (AAB), SDK traffic or all Google services.

4. Device narration

The invitation to call an adult on the purchase screen may be narrated using device text-to-speech (TTS), when a voice compatible with the selected language is available. The game provides the invitation text to the voice engine selected by the system. The provider configured by the family may process it locally or use online services, depending on its settings and policy; we therefore do not claim that all TTS works offline. Game music and sound effects play from files included in the app. The narration does not record the child’s voice.

5. Children's data

We do not request a child’s name, email address, age, photo, location or registration. The parental challenge separates purchase decisions from the child’s activity, and resetting progress requires confirmation intended for an adult. Technical purchase data associated with the store account is described in section 3; voluntary contact by parents and guardians is described in section 12.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. The game does not send local progress or preferences to the studio. Google Play Billing and the device’s configured TTS provider handle information needed for the functions described in sections 3 and 4 under their own practices. Email support and visits to the document website are separate activities described in sections 11 and 12.

7. Security

Progress and preferences stay in the app’s local data area; their protection also depends on device updates, protections and access controls. The app code review did not identify transmission of these files or the purchase token to a server owned by the studio; external services are described in sections 3 and 4. The document website uses HTTPS. Support messages are transmitted and stored by the email providers involved under those services’ protections.

8. Retention

Progress and preferences remain on the device while the app’s files are kept. Resetting progress replaces stage progress with the initial state and keeps preferences. Clearing app storage/data in Android settings removes its local files. Uninstalling and any restoration of system backups depend on device settings. The studio does not keep a copy of progress. Store, voice-provider, hosting and support-message records follow the separate handling described in this policy.

9. Deletion

In Settings, “Reset progress” requires a correct answer to an arithmetic question and confirmation before returning to stage 1 and clearing completed stages. Language, volume and other settings remain saved. This action does not cancel or delete the purchase linked to the Google Play account. To also remove preferences and other local files, use the clear app storage/data option in Android settings. The studio cannot remotely erase device data. Data held by Google Play and other providers must be managed through those services’ channels.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services, or legal requirements change. The date at the top will be revised and material changes will appear in a new app version.

11. Visits to the document website

This document website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not require login. Its code does not add ads, usage analytics, cookies or browser storage for preferences. When visiting its pages, your browser communicates with the hosting service. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, even without login. This handling is separate from the game’s operation and follows GitHub’s policies and retention periods.

12. Contact and support messages

Parents and guardians can send privacy or support questions to vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com. Sending a message provides your email address and whatever content you choose to send; this data is not collected during play. We use the information to respond to the request and retain it for as long as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. Gmail, used for support, and your email provider handle messages under their policies. Avoid sending a child’s name, photo or other personal information unless needed to explain the issue. Use the same contact to request information, correction or deletion of submitted data, subject to any retention obligations. There is no game account to delete.
