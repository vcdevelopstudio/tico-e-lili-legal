Game and supervision

Tico e Lili : Encantos do Folclore offers children's activities, an illustrated book and a painting studio. An adult should supervise purchases and visits to external pages. The arithmetic gate does not verify age or identity.

Free and paid content

Adventures 1–3 are free. One non-consumable purchase unlocks access to adventures 4–10 without a subscription. Google Play presents the applicable price and conditions before confirmation. Purchased access does not complete activities or immediately unlock the art studio; the studio requires completing adventure 10. No specific educational outcome is promised.

Purchase and restoration

Payments, confirmation, restoration and refunds follow the applicable Google Play services and rules. Restoration requires the purchasing account and internet. The game validates receipts and may maintain a local access record; this does not replace the store's records or service availability. Refunds or revocations may affect access. Restoring a purchase does not transfer progress or drawings between devices.

Drawings and progress

Progress and drawings are local. Families can edit or delete works in the gallery. Campaign reset preserves the gallery and purchased access; uninstalling or clearing data can remove local files. The studio provides no cloud synchronization or recovery. There is no in-app online sharing of drawings.

Rights and licenses

The Tico and Lili visual identity, code, original illustrations and music are subject to applicable rights and the licenses included with the app. This does not claim exclusive rights over Brazilian folklore legends or traditions. Third-party libraries and fonts retain their licenses.
