VC Develop Studio

1. Scope and contact

This policy describes data handling in Tico e Lili : Encantos do Folclore, Android package com.engvcdevelop.ticoelili.folclore. For support or privacy questions, contact vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com. Information voluntarily sent to support is used to respond to the request. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information about a child.

2. Children and gameplay

The game is intended for children aged 3–6, with family supervision of adult areas. It has no in-app account, chat, advertisements, voice recording, camera or location feature. The arithmetic gate is a navigation barrier for adults; it does not verify legal age, identity or consent. We do not integrate our own gameplay behavioral analytics service. Google purchase services have separate data practices described below.

3. On-device information

Progress, language and volume preferences, book pages, keepsakes and their positions are saved locally. Drawings, compositions and thumbnails that the family chooses to save in the art studio also remain on the device, with local recovery files. The studio does not provide a cloud account or synchronization and the game does not upload drawings to us. Music, sound effects and book narration are included audio files.

4. One-time purchase and restoration

Adventures 1–3 are free. One non-consumable purchase provides access to adventures 4–10, without a subscription. Activities must still be completed in sequence; buying does not complete them. The art studio unlocks after adventure 10 is completed. Google Play presents the localized price before confirmation. Google Play processes payments; the app does not receive payment-card details. The integration receives product, purchase state, token, acknowledgement and signed receipt information to query, acknowledge and restore access. The app stores a signed purchase receipt locally, including store-returned information needed to validate it, which may include a token and order identifier. These records are separate from progress. The studio has no receipt-validation backend. Purchases, price queries and restoration require internet and store services. Restoration depends on the Google account used for the purchase; it does not automatically recover progress or drawings from another device.

5. Google service diagnostics

The commercial version integrates GodotGooglePlayBilling 3.3.0 and Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0. The SDK includes operational diagnostics and event transport to Google, with a persistent local DataTransport/CCT queue. Technical information includes connection, success and failure events, a random library instance/session identifier, app/SDK/Android package and versions, device brand, model, manufacturer, memory and system build identifier. A technical session identifier is not the AAID advertising identifier, IMEI or hardware serial number, and does not establish anonymity. Billing starts when the commercial app opens, before the adult area and independently of a purchase. The game has no setting to disable these diagnostics; choosing not to purchase does not disable them. Persistent queues are not memory-only processing. Technical diagnostics are separate from gameplay analytics. Google handles its service data under its Privacy Policy. We do not promise a specific retention period or deletion from Google's systems.

Privacy Policy

6. Recipients, security and website

We do not sell children's data or send drawings and progress to a studio server. Store-service information and SDK diagnostics may be sent to Google for operation, purchasing, restoration and technical diagnostics. The absence of advertisements does not remove these flows. External services use their protected connections; no system offers an absolute security guarantee. Public documents are hosted on GitHub Pages. When someone opens the website, the provider receives ordinary connection information such as an IP address and may retain it for security under its policies. Browser visits are separate from local progress storage. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.

7. Retention and deletion

Drawings can be deleted in the art-studio gallery. Resetting progress restarts the campaign while preserving preferences, saved drawings and purchased access. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local files; it does not cancel purchases or erase Google's records. The commercial app disables its automatic Android backup and the studio provides no cloud recovery. For Google-handled data, also use Google's controls and privacy channels. Support can explain local files and address requests concerning data actually received by the studio.
