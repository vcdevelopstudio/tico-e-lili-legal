Privacy Policy

Draft dated October 10, 2026 — future purchase version; not effective.

Publisher: VC Develop Studio

1. Scope of this policy

This draft Privacy Policy was prepared for the future version of Tico e Lili: Mistérios do Fundo do Mar with Google Play Billing purchases. It is addressed to parents and guardians. The game includes underwater activities, a discovery book and a musical bonus, in Portuguese, English and Spanish. The current development version does not yet integrate purchases. This draft must be checked and updated against the final commercial integration before taking effect.

2. Children and personal data

The game does not ask children to register or provide their name, email, identity document, date of birth, photo or location. Activities do not use the camera or microphone. The current project has no advertising, chat, studio-managed account, studio server or behavioral analytics integration for play. The future purchase integration involves data processing by Google services, described in sections 5 and 6; absence of advertising does not mean absence of third-party collection. Parents and guardians should supervise device use and purchase decisions.

3. Progress and preferences on the device

The game stores the language, music, effects and narration volumes, completed stages, revealed keepsakes and read book pages locally. The device language guides the initial choice of Portuguese, English or Spanish; if it is none of these, English is selected initially. Manual choices are saved. These records let families continue the journey and keep preferences. The game retains a previous local copy for recovery if the main file has a problem. The studio does not receive these files or offer progress synchronization between devices.

4. Audio and music created in the bonus

Music, effects and narration play from files included in the app. The game does not use an online text-to-speech service to narrate the book. In the musical bonus, “Record” stores the notes played on the shells, their sequence, timing and the composition duration, up to 30 seconds; it does not record voices or ambient sounds. Songs the family chooses to save, their local identifiers and optional titles remain in a library separate from progress, with a local recovery copy. These records are not sent to the studio and the game has no online sharing feature. Avoid using children's names or other personal data in song titles.

5. Google Play purchases and restoration

The planned model provides stages 1–3 free of charge and a one-time, non-consumable purchase for access to stages 4–10, without a subscription or recurring charges. Access remains subject to progression through completed stages. Google Play processes payment. The planned integration will use product and transaction information, such as localized price, product identifier, purchase state, purchase token and acknowledgment status, to query, confirm or restore access and acknowledge the transaction with the store. The studio does not request card details. Purchases, queries and restoration may require a connection and the Google Play account used for purchase. Storage of access rights and transaction metadata, including any tokens, must be described according to the final implementation; this draft does not state that an access cache, server verification or token storage exists today. The planned numerical challenge before the purchase area is a barrier intended for a responsible adult, not legal verification of age, identity or consent.

6. Google services and technical diagnostics

The future billing library may process and send Google technical app and device data and identifiers for operations or the library instance for diagnostics and service operation. This processing must be checked in the version actually integrated, including startup before a purchase, automatic transmission, a persistent local queue and any ability to disable it. In the reference games, Billing 9.1.0 uses diagnostic transport and a local queue; this configuration is not automatically confirmed for this game. We do not promise anonymization, memory-only processing or absence of diagnostics when a family does not purchase. Before publication, this section must state the actual data, purposes, recipients and collection conditions of the selected integration. Google processes its service data under its own Privacy Policy. The system and store may also process installation, updates and backups according to their settings.

Google Privacy Policy

7. Retention and deletion of local data

In Settings, “Reset progress” requires the correct answer to a numerical challenge and confirmation. It resets stage progress, keepsakes and read pages; the language, volumes and bonus music library remain. Answering the challenge does not legally verify age or identity. Resetting may retain the previous local progress copy. In the bonus library, deleting a song removes it from the active list after confirmation; a previous file copy may remain on the device. To remove preferences, songs, progress and local copies, use the app's clear storage/data option in Android settings. Uninstallation and any system restoration depend on device settings. The studio cannot remotely delete or recover these files. In the purchase version, resetting progress or removing local files does not cancel a purchase in the Google Play account. The relationship between reset, access cache and restoration must be checked in the final integration. Local removal does not automatically delete purchase records or diagnostics already sent to Google; these are managed through that service's channels.

8. Sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Progress, preferences and bonus compositions stay in the app data area, whose protection also depends on the system, updates and device controls. Future purchases use Google Play services. Email support and visits to this document website are separate processing activities, explained in sections 9 and 10. We do not promise absolute security or a retention or deletion period for data controlled by third parties.

9. Contact and privacy rights

The publisher is VC Develop Studio. Parents and guardians may contact vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com for support, privacy information and requests to access, correct or delete data sent to the studio, under applicable law. When you send a message, you provide your email address and chosen content; we use this information to handle the request and retain it for the time needed for support and applicable legal obligations. Gmail used for support and your email provider process messages under their policies. Avoid sending children's names, photos or other personal data unnecessary to explain the problem. There is no studio-managed game account to delete. The studio holds no copy of progress or songs to recover or delete remotely.

10. Visits to the document website

The document website uses GitHub Pages and requires no login. Its code adds no advertising, usage analytics, cookies or preference storage in the browser. Opening a page connects your browser to the hosting provider. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, even without login. This processing is separate from game operation and follows GitHub's policies. Links to these documents open the browser and may require a connection.

11. Changes and effective status

This draft does not announce purchase availability or publication of a new game version. It will be reviewed before the commercial version to match the services and flows actually used. After taking effect, the policy may be updated when the game, its services or applicable requirements change; the date at the beginning will indicate the revision.
