Privacy Policy

Updated October 20, 2026

Publisher: VC Develop Studio

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy describes Tico e Lili: Mistérios do Fundo do Mar commercial version 0.1.0, code 5, prepared for closed testing, with Google Play Billing 9.1.0 integration. It is addressed to parents and guardians. The game includes underwater activities, a discovery book and a musical bonus, in Portuguese, English and Spanish. Describing this version does not announce Google approval or game availability on Play. The “More games” catalog remains disabled in the configuration checked on October 20, 2026; the flows in that section apply only when the feature is enabled.

2. Children and personal data

The game does not ask children to register or provide their name, email, identity document, date of birth, photo or location. Activities do not use the camera or microphone. There is no third-party advertising, chat, studio-managed account, studio server, advertising SDK or behavioral analytics SDK for play. Google Play Billing processes purchase information and technical diagnostics as described in sections 5 and 6, including before the adult area and without a purchase. The mathematical challenge is a navigation barrier intended for adults; it does not legally verify age, identity or consent and is not a neutral age screen. Parents and guardians should supervise device use and use Google Play account controls.

3. Progress and preferences on the device

The game stores the language, music, effects and narration volumes, completed stages, revealed keepsakes and read book pages locally. The device language guides the initial choice of Portuguese, English or Spanish; if it is none of these, English is selected initially. Manual choices are saved. These records let families continue the journey and keep preferences. The game retains a previous local copy for recovery if the main file has a problem. The studio does not receive these files or offer progress synchronization between devices.

4. Audio and music created in the bonus

Music, effects and narration play from files included in the app. The game does not use an online text-to-speech service to narrate the book. In the musical bonus, “Record” stores the notes played on the shells, their sequence, timing and the composition duration, up to 30 seconds; it does not record voices or ambient sounds. Songs the family chooses to save, their local identifiers and optional titles remain in a library separate from progress, with a local recovery copy. These records are not sent to the studio and the game has no online sharing feature. Avoid using children's names or other personal data in song titles.

5. Google Play purchases and restoration

Stages 1–3 are free of charge. A permanent, one-time, non-consumable purchase grants access rights to stages 4–10 without a subscription or recurring charges. Purchase preserves progression: it does not automatically complete stages or reveal keepsakes or book pages. The musical bonus requires completion of stage 10. The localized price is queried from Google Play and shown before confirmation; this policy sets no universal price. Google Play processes payment; the app does not access card details. The integration receives the product, purchase state and token, and acknowledgment status to query access rights and acknowledge the transaction (ACK) with the store. Tokens and states returned by Play are handled in memory for these operations; the game's own cache does not store tokens, accounts, email, card data or order identifiers. There is no studio server verification. The commercial version starts Billing and catalog and ownership queries on app startup, queries again on return and queries Google Play when the adult selects “Restore”. Purchasing, prices, queries and restoration require a connection and store services; recovering access depends on the account used for purchase. The app's memory-only handling does not mean that Google processes all records ephemerally. Purchase history and information serve purchase and restoration functionality, in connection with an optional purchase or a query for an existing entitlement. In the Data safety form, expected transmission for a user-initiated action may fall under that action's sharing exception; this does not mean no transmission to Google and does not automatically apply to SDK diagnostics.

6. Google services and technical diagnostics

The commercial version integrates GodotGooglePlayBilling 3.3.0 and Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0. Static inspection of the SDK and final package identified the PLAY_BILLING_LIBRARY diagnostic mechanism and DataTransport/CCT transport. It records operational success, failure and connection events for diagnostics, performance and service operation, forwarded to Google. The mechanism includes a random identifier per library instance/session, app package, SDK version, Android version, brand, model, manufacturer, memory information and system build fingerprint (Build.FINGERPRINT, not the Build.SERIAL serial number). The session identifier is not an advertising identifier (AAID), IMEI or persistent Android ID; it does not guarantee anonymity. DataTransport 3.1.8 maintains a persistent local SQLite queue for later transmission; this processing is therefore not memory-only or ephemeral. Billing starts before the parental area and without a purchase. The game provides no option to disable these diagnostics, and not purchasing does not disable them. Diagnostics and technical identifiers are separate from progress and play analytics; encoding and transport dependencies do not amount to Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. This description is based on static inspection of a reachable mechanism, not complete traffic capture or an audit of Google servers. Anonymization, retention periods and automatic deletion at Google have not been confirmed. Classification as Diagnostics and Device or other IDs, automatically collected, required and non-ephemeral, for functionality and technical analytics, is a reasoned interpretation of Data safety rules rather than specific Google approval. Forwarding to Google is separate from a user-initiated purchase; we do not automatically apply the studio service-provider exception to diagnostics. Google processes its service data under its Privacy Policy.

7. Local purchased-access cache

Separately from progress and songs, the app maintains a local access-rights cache with the product identifier, app package, environment and a boolean purchased-access flag (owned). This file contains no token, account or email, card data or order identifier. It retains the local access state for the code to read; it is not a copy of the Google Play account and does not synchronize progress. Store queries reconcile this state: a valid response may change access, while a network failure does not prove absence of a purchase. Having a cache does not establish validated offline restart. Purchasing, price queries and restoration require a network connection; we do not guarantee purchased access after an offline restart before physical validation. The cache remains until its state is updated or local data is removed.

8. Retention and deletion of local data

In Settings, “Reset progress” requires a correct numerical answer and confirmation. It resets stages, keepsakes and read pages while preserving language, volumes, the music library and purchase rights. A previous local progress copy may remain. Deleting a bonus song removes it from the active list after confirmation, but a previous copy may remain. “Restore purchase” queries Google Play to recover access rights; it does not restore deleted progress or songs. To remove preferences, songs, progress, copies, the access cache and local queues, use clear storage/data in Android settings. This does not cancel the purchase in the Play account; recovering access requires a new store query/restoration. The Release version disables automatic app backup (allowBackup=false); we offer no studio-server synchronization or file recovery. Uninstalling and clearing data may cause loss of local files. Local removal does not delete purchase records or diagnostics already sent to Google. The studio cannot remotely delete files on the device or records on Google servers; data processed by Google must be managed through its channels and policies, without promises of an unconfirmed deletion deadline.

9. Sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Progress, preferences, compositions and the cache stay in the app data area, whose protection also depends on the system, updates and device controls. Purchase information and diagnostics described in sections 5 and 6 are processed by Google services. Email support and website visits are separate activities, described in sections 10 and 11. We do not promise absolute security or third-party data retention or deletion within an unconfirmed period.

10. Contact and privacy rights

The publisher is VC Develop Studio. Parents and guardians may contact vcdevelopstudio@gmail.com for support, privacy information and requests to access, correct or delete data sent to the studio, under applicable law. When you send a message, you provide your email address and chosen content; we use this information to handle the request and retain it for the time needed for support and applicable legal obligations. Gmail used for support and your email provider process messages under their policies. Avoid sending children's names, photos or other personal data unnecessary to explain the problem. There is no studio-managed game account to delete. The studio holds no copy of progress or songs to recover or delete remotely.

11. Visits to the document website

The document website uses GitHub Pages and requires no login. Its code adds no advertising, usage analytics, cookies or preference storage in the browser. Opening a page connects your browser to the hosting provider. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, even without login. This processing is separate from game operation and follows GitHub's policies. Links to these documents open the browser and may require a connection.

12. More games catalog images and store

The “More games” remote catalog remains disabled in the Mar configuration checked on October 20, 2026: that configuration makes no feed requests or icon downloads. Content is maintained by VC Develop Studio, and the designated hosting is GitHub Pages through GitHub's delivery infrastructure at vcdevelopstudio.github.io. The endpoint is https://vcdevelopstudio.github.io/tico-e-lili-legal/catalogo/v1/jogos.json; icons use the same host under /tico-e-lili-legal/catalogo/v1/icons/. This policy does not enable the feature or announce a version already offering it. When enabled, this will be an optional area for discovering other apps from the same publisher. Opening the list or tapping Refresh will request the feed and download icons over HTTPS when needed; these requests will not occur automatically on entering the initial menu or during stages. Downloads will precede the parental gate for opening the store, so they will not be exclusively adult traffic. Hosting will receive the source IP, requested path and technical headers to deliver files and protect the service. GitHub states that it logs and stores IPs for security, even without login; these records are not processed only in memory. We have no confirmed specific retention period for Pages logs or guarantee of anonymization or immediate deletion. GitHub's general policy ties retention to purposes and applicable obligations; we assign no unconfirmed numerical period to these logs. The catalog code adds no cookies, authentication, personal, device or advertising identifiers, purchase tokens, progress, challenge results or impression/click events to requests. We do not use the catalog to determine a child's location. Absence of usage analytics or a studio click server does not eliminate hosting technical records. The separate catalog cache stores the public list, revision and integrity hash, acceptance time, invalidation flag and icons by hash. It contains no progress, license, Billing tokens or parental authorization. Content may be used only while valid: up to the earlier of feed expiry and 24 hours after local acceptance. This display limit is not a file deletion or remote log retention period. Files and the marker against replaying older revisions may remain after expiry; resetting progress does not clear them. Clearing app storage/data removes this local cache without deleting GitHub or Google records. We do not promise an offline catalog, continuous availability or instantaneous withdrawal of an offer. Opening another app's listing requires an explicit choice, a correct parental challenge answer and revalidation of the chosen destination and revision. Canceling, answering incorrectly or losing selection validity does not open the store; each action requires its own authorization. The challenge does not legally verify age, identity or consent. Mar's voice invitation uses files included in the app in the configuration examined. The system requests opening the official Google Play listing or the browser; those services process the visit under their own policies. There is no installation, automatic purchase or reward for visiting another app. Requests concerning hosting records must follow GitHub's privacy channels; the studio cannot remotely delete them. In-game catalog downloads and browser visits to the documents in section 11 are separate flows, even with the same hosting.
